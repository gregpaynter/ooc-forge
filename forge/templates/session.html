Video Experience

Direct the Seed through time.

Forge retains the original creative prompt, derives temporal direction with the local LLM, then adds your optional direction before producing the resolved shot plan.

Audio Experience

Give the Work a soundtrack.

Stable Audio 3 uses the Seed Work's creative prompt plus your optional audio direction. Audio does not depend on Qwen. A lossless master is always retained.

When linked to video, Forge uses that video's duration and resolved temporal direction, then muxes the generated soundtrack into the existing video without re-rendering Wan2.2. Manage audio model.
